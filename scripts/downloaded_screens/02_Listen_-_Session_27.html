SESSION 27

Match tone to message weight.

0:32 / 1:45

When we talk about executive presence, we often default to volume. But volume is a blunt instrument.

Using energy variance as a deliberate signal commands attention far more effectively than sustained loudness.

Think of a drop in pitch and a slowing of pace just before delivering a critical metric. It physically forces the room to lean in. It signals: "This carries weight."
