Tidbit

"Conclusion plus three supports"

01:14

Effective communication often breaks down because we present information in the order we discovered it, not the order the audience needs to understand it.

The Barbara Minto Pyramid Principle flips this script: you must start with the answer first.

Deliver your main conclusion immediately, then group and summarize your supporting arguments. Ideally, you want three strong supporting points. Any more, and the audience loses track. Any fewer, and your argument lacks stability.

Think of it like building a structure. The conclusion is the peak, visible from far away. The three supports are the solid foundation holding it up. This forces clarity in your own thinking before you even begin to speak.
