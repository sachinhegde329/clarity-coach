Session 32

Executive Presence

Authority signals in voice. Learning presence through restraint.

0:42
1:15

Transcript

When we think about executive presence, we often imagine speaking louder or taking up more physical space.

True presence often manifests as restraint: a measured pace, downward inflection at the end of statements, and the confidence to hold comfortable pauses.

Notice how dropping your pitch slightly at the conclusion of a thought signals finality and certainty.
