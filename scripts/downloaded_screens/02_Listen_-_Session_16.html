COMBINING CLARITY TOOLS

notes TRANSCRIPT

When you begin to apply multiple constraints simultaneously, the cognitive load increases exponentially. This is intentional.

The difficulty of stacking is real. The tactical tip: structure mentally before speaking.

If you try to improvise while holding three different rules in your mind, your pacing will collapse. Pause. Build the architecture of your sentence, then deliver it cleanly.
