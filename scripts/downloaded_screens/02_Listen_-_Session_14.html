LIVE TRANSCRIPT description

Why exactly does the number three hold such weight in human communication?

The answer lies in cognitive load. Research suggests that three is the smallest number required to create a pattern, and the largest number that most people can store in their short-term memory instantly.

When you present four points, you risk losing the listener. When you present two, you lack the structural integrity of a "narrative arc."

Think of the stool: three legs provide perfect balance. Communication is no different.

In this session, we will explore how to ruthlessly prune your ideas down to the core triad that defines your message.
