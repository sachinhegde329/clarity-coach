Every effective business narrative follows a four-beat structure. We call this the Setup, the Complication, the Resolution, and the Lesson.

00:45

Let's focus on the Setup. You need to establish the baseline normal before the disruption hits. Without a clear picture of 'before', the 'after' has no impact.

01:15

Then comes the Complication. The inciting incident that forces change. This is where your customer realizes their current solution is broken...

01:40

The Resolution is how your product or service navigated them out of the complication. It's the journey back to stability.
