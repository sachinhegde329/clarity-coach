Mirroring and Labelling

Concept Briefing

0:14 / 1:30

Effective communication relies on making the other party feel understood.

Before jumping into problem-solving, we must establish a baseline of empathy.

"Mirroring is simply repeating the last three words of what someone said with a curious downward tone. It signals you are listening without judging."

This subtle technique buys you time to think and encourages them to elaborate.

Following a mirror, you might use a label to identify the emotion driving their statement.
